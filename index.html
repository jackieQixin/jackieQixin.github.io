About Me

I am a PhD candidate at the National University of Singapore, advised by Prof. Lin Shao. My research focuses on embodied AI, World Action Models (WAM), and real-world reinforcement learning for loco-manipulation.

My journey with robotics began at Chongqing University, where I built robots ranging from rehabilitation robots to quadruped robots. I later dove deep into reinforcement learning under the supervision of Prof. Chao Huang at the University of Southampton, and collaborated with Prof. Donglin Wang and MiLab at Westlake University on humanoid robots, VLA, and world models.

My long-term vision is to develop lifelong-learning embodied agents adaptable to real-world settings — robots that can enter ordinary homes to deliver elderly care and uphold human dignity, especially by supporting marginalized groups.

We are looking for collaborators. If you take ownership and aspire to produce meaningful work, please get in touch. Relevant prior experience is not mandatory — we can grow together!
